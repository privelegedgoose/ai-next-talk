Step 3  ·  Architecture

Architecture can look different. This was mine.

Option A
Autonomous agents

An AI agent explores the files, decides and acts on its own.

Flexible. Harder to control, check and explain.

Option B  ·  My choice
Files + rules + one AI call

The app fetches the right files. The AI adapts them under written rules.

Predictable. Cheap. Easy to check.

For a first version inside a company, I choose the design where quality is easier to control.
